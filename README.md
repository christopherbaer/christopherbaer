# Christopher Baer

Building things on the internet, with interests at the intersection of technology, science, and medicine.

I'm a product and strategy leader. My career spans eBay, Tripadvisor (FlipKey), and Wish. At Wish, I served as Sr. Director of Strategy & Transformation and led product for on-site advertising and the desktop and mobile web experience.

I studied Human Biology, with a concentration in Neuroscience and a minor in Mathematical & Computational Science, at Stanford University. I'm based in Silicon Valley.

## What I'm building

- [Poet Doctor](https://poetdoctor.com) — An independent medical humanities research initiative studying medicine as a human practice. The work includes historical medical texts and translations, physician poetry, digital analysis, parallel charts, and inquiry into how AI may change clinical judgment and physicians' responsibilities.
- [Med to Tech](https://medtotech.com) (in alpha) — A career transition platform helping clinicians move from medicine into technology.
- [baeronline.com](https://baeronline.com) — My personal website, built with Astro and deployed on Cloudflare Workers.

My repositories and contribution history below reflect the ongoing work behind these projects.
